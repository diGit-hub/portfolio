# Favicon do portfólio

Imagem de origem: `src/assets/gabriel.jpg`.

A adaptação foi feita com a ferramenta integrada ImageGen, preservando o rosto e os óculos, aproximando o enquadramento e simplificando o fundo. Os arquivos finais foram exportados com ImageMagick.

- `public/favicon.png`: retrato circular de 256 × 256 px, com transparência.
- `public/favicon-16x16.png` e `public/favicon-32x32.png`: ícones para abas do navegador.
- `public/favicon.ico`: tamanhos 16, 32, 48 e 64 px.
- `public/apple-touch-icon.png`: ícone de 180 × 180 px com fundo opaco.

O HTML usa `%BASE_URL%` para funcionar também em subdiretórios e uma versão na URL para atualizar o cache do ícone.

## Prompt utilizado

Use case: identity-preserve. Asset type: personal portfolio website favicon, one square portrait asset. Input image: edit target, the provided original photograph of Gabriel in a white shirt, dark tie and silver glasses. Primary request: adapt this exact photograph into a clean photographic avatar optimized for very small browser favicon sizes. Preserve his actual face, facial proportions, skin tone, short curly black hair, thin silver glasses and neutral expression faithfully; do not invent another person and do not beautify. Composition: centered, very tightly framed head and only a little white shirt collar at bottom, hair nearly touches top, whole chin visible, face/head occupies roughly 85 percent of image height. Remove the outdoor background, hand, wristwatch and torso. Use a simple flat very pale blue-gray circular backing behind the head, with transparent corners outside the circle. Keep the circle almost edge to edge, minimal padding. Natural photo texture, clear silhouette, crisp hair and glasses, balanced contrast for readability at 16x16 and 32x32. No lettering, no initials, no border, no logo, no watermark, no 3D mockup, no multiple variants.
